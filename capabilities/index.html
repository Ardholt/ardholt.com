What we build.

Capabilities

The fields Ardholt works in. Each is a kind of system the practice has conceived, built and run in its entirety.

01

Voice and telephony

Systems that speak.

Voice on live telephone lines, in real time, with the composure to be interrupted and the judgement to listen as well as talk. The phone, answered to a higher standard than the person who used to answer it.

What this carriesLive telephony, speech in and out, interruption, measured quality.

02

Autonomous agent systems

Systems that work.

Agents that take a brief and return finished work: planned, built, proven and delivered without a hand on the wheel, and a person brought in only where a decision is genuinely theirs to make.

What this carriesPlanning, building, proving and delivering, with gates and reach-out.

03

Knowledge and retrieval

Systems that remember.

An organisation's knowledge, structured, kept current and answerable, so that what it knows can be found in seconds, trusted on sight and acted on.

What this carriesStructured knowledge, retrieval, upkeep, trust.

04

Operator consoles and messaging

Systems that converse.

Operations run from the channels people already use. Work is commissioned, questioned and delivered in plain language, by a system that reports the way a good colleague would.

What this carriesIntake, clarification, progress and delivery on the client's channels.

05

Platforms and products

Systems people use.

Products and interfaces finished to the standard people expect from the best software they own, and engineered to outlast the tools of the year they were built in.

What this carriesProducts, portals, interfaces, built on standards.

06

Data and automation

Systems that run themselves.

The work that should never have been done by hand, taken over by pipelines that are trusted precisely because they never guess and never go quiet.

What this carriesPipelines, validation, traceability, exceptions named.

07

Infrastructure and compute

Systems that stay up.

Private compute and GPU infrastructure across sites, built to run unattended, recover on its own and carry models that matter, without a cloud bill or a cloud dependency.

What this carriesPrivate compute, GPU estates, recovery, operation without attendance.

08

Security, evaluation and assurance

Systems that prove themselves.

Security designed in from the first line, and evaluation that attacks every release with planted faults before anyone else has the chance. Nothing ships on assurance alone.

What this carriesSecurity designed in, planted-fault evaluation, independent verification.
