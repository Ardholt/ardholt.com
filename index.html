The standard

How every engagement runs.

The same discipline on every system, whatever its size. It is what makes a fixed price and an agreed date possible, and it is why the work can be relied upon afterwards.

Scope, in writing, first

What will be built and how it will be judged are agreed before work begins. The acceptance criteria are the contract.

Built in isolation, proven against the criteria

Work happens in its own environment, tested against the agreed criteria as it goes, never against a demo.

Delivered with evidence

Every delivery arrives with a hand-off note on what changed and why, and the test evidence that each criterion was met.

Accountable after hand-over

Ardholt stands behind what it delivers. What is within the agreed scope is made right.
